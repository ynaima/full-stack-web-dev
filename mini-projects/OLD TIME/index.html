Memories Of Old Times

In the quiet corners of my mind, I often find myself wandering through the corridors of time, reminiscing about the old times. The games we used to play, the songs we used to sing, and the moments we used to share. Let me take you on a journey down memory lane.

Childhood Games

Double Dutch

Jump in rhythm—time your steps between two swinging ropes without getting caught.

Hopscotch

Toss, hop, and balance—move through the squares on one foot and grab your marker.

Stack & Remove Battery

Carefully pull and steady—remove pieces without making the stack collapse.

Paper Folding (Origami)

Fold and create—turn simple paper into planes, boats, and fun designs.

Grid Hop

Jump, balance, and guide your stone through the grid without missing a step.

Power Jump

Test your strength and timing as each round pushes you to jump further than before.
